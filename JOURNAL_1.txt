2. Now to decide what microcontroller, I already have 3

![20260316_220758](https://stasis.hackclub-assets.com/images/1773724149938-nkh8f7.jpg)

 - ESP32-WROOM32-WIFI: runs on 3v3, has wifi, a load of pins, any protocol one could ever want
 - EIMTechnology's STEPico (essentially a Pico with 4 neopixel leds in pin 23): runs on 3v3, medium amount of pins, easy micropython, any protocol
 - Arduino Nano: runs on 5v :), loads of ADC PINS unlike the top 2, less pins overall though

I chose the biggest and baddest, the esp32, it is generally what is used for multi module projects like this, 
and it has the most gpio pins, and most pins overall
and it is the only one out of these that has any inbuilt wifi support.

3. This specific board that I have is the esp32 wroom32 wifi, 
but it barely has any compatible documentation or footprints for it, if it exists, its for either the chip only, another esp32 board, a different wroom32 board with more or less chips than 30 (which is what I have)

After a lot of red herrings, I finally found a compatible footprint, from a doit_esp32_devboard, which isnt close to my wroom32
but It has the right number of pins and has them in the right places ( I checked everysingle one )
