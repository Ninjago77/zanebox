So, I got the chance to go to a Moderately Local Electronics Shop, & I was able to procure
some essentials and backups for the project:-

![image](https://stasis.hackclub-assets.com/images/1775582785026-jdat7f.jpg)

 - The 18 gauge solid core is for direct connection from the USB Breakouts to the PCB
 - The 90 degree headers for 16pin connections between both boards
 - the Female extended headers are for gaps between the pcb & the displays & also to squeeze the microcontrollers underneath.
